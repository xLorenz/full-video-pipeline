# crowd

Crowd room tone (CC0 sample, ~5s): a speaking/shouting crowd murmur — a stadium, a protest,
"millions of people" statistics.

Moods: serious, tense, upbeat

## When to use
- "millions of users / people" beats        →  when: "start" (bed-like, volume 0.2-0.3)
- social-scale statistics, market/arena scenes
- establishing "the world outside" moments

## When NOT to use
- at full volume under narration — use it as a quiet bed
- in intimate one-on-one scenes (a crowd breaks intimacy)

## Parameters
None (sample playback; use `volume` to set bed presence).

## Examples
{ "sound": "crowd", "when": "start", "volume": 0.2 }
