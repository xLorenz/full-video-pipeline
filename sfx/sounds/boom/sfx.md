# boom

Deep sub boom, ~1.5s — the biggest, lowest hit in the catalog. Big reveals, chapter breaks,
logo moments. Presence is felt more than heard; keep it sparse.

Moods: serious, tense, neutral

## When to use
- chapter break / part open                      →  when: "start" (of the opening scene)
- a huge statistic or reveal lands
- title-card or brand moment

## When NOT to use
- more than once per video chapter (it marks *breaks*, not beats)
- in calm scenes — `thud` covers gentle landings

## Parameters
| param | range | default | meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| pitch | 0.5 - 2.0 | 1.0 | pitch multiplier (1.3+ = shallower, faster boom; 0.8 = deeper) |

## Examples
{ "sound": "boom", "when": "start", "volume": 0.6 }
