# bounce

Rubber-ball bounce, ~0.7s: four hits (420 -> 285 -> 215 -> 172 Hz) with
pitch-drop each hit, tightening spacing, plus a soft strike tick on the first.
Reads as "boing", playful entrance, energy settling down.

Moods: playful, upbeat, humorous, neutral

## When to use
- playful entrances, characters hopping in        when: "beat:bounce"
- "wobble" / physics-gag moments
- countdown endings that bounce, not drop (`boom` drops)

## When NOT to use
- for serious/tense content — use `thud` / `stamp`
- over narration: the multi-hit tail (0.7s) needs room to read
