# stamp

Authority stamp, ~0.25s: low thump sweeping 130 to 55 Hz, a band-passed
paper-slap transient, a metallic 247 Hz clunk, and a low 85 Hz body settle.
Reads as "approved", "final answer", "sealed".

Moods: serious, tense, playful, neutral

## When to use
- verdicts, final answers, "case closed"          when: "beat:verdict"
- stamps/seals landing on graphics
- heavier than `thud`, sharper than `boom`

## When NOT to use
- for soft/calm moments — `thud` is the gentle alternative
- under the loudest narration: the slap transient is bright (700-4000 Hz band)

none
