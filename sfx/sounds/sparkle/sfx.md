# sparkle

Random twinkles, ~1.2s: several high sine blips at randomized pitch/timing — a soft
celebration confetti of sound.

Moods: playful, upbeat, humorous

## When to use
- celebration moments ("and they lived happily ever after")
- confetti / trophy / win screens
- humorous accents in comedic beats

## When NOT to use
- **in serious or tense videos — a sparkle there is a tone error; validation will warn.** A
  sparkle in a "data breach" scene destroys the mood.
- as a generic "nice" accent — `shimmer` is the elegant alternative

## Parameters
None — randomness is seeded and deterministic per cue.

## Examples
{ "sound": "sparkle", "when": "beat:confetti", "volume": 0.35 }
